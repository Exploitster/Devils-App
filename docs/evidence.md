# Reviewed evidence

The production collection is `data/evidence/seed.json`. It contains nine source records with twelve exact excerpts: RethinkDB's founder announcement, Atom's official README, Mill's On Liberty chapters II/III, Mill's Utilitarianism chapter II, Aurelius' Meditations, Fourth Book X in this Casaubon edition, two Google ADK evaluation-documentation records, and NIST Dioptra's experiment-provenance description. The original sources were inspected on 2026-10-14; the three AI-evaluation records were fetched and inspected with normal TLS verification on 2026-10-15, then reviewed against the production schema. This is source inspection by Codex, not a claim of outside expert or human editorial review.

The pinned HTTPS source URL, source SHA-256, exact excerpt SHA-256, source/edition locator, licensing constraints, review identity/timestamps, supported claim and interpretation classification travel with every record. `inspection.json` maps each record to a complete local source and license snapshot. Universal newline normalization to LF is the only normalization applied; quote strings retain their exact stored line breaks. Electronic release/posting dates are distinguished from historical print publication in `edition`; no page numbers are inferred. Empty business metadata fields mean unknown.

## Coverage

RethinkDB supports its announced shutdown and inability to build a sustainable business, and the announced availability of RethinkDB/Horizon as open source. It does not establish specific pricing, demand, distribution or competition causes. Geography and an exact founding date are unknown. Atom is an optional product-retirement announcement, **not a failed startup**; the README does not establish realized archival, economic causes or a publication date. GitHub did not shut down.

Business coverage is limited to software/developer infrastructure and narrowly supported AI-evaluation practices. Generic business/customer/payer vocabulary cannot retrieve these cases. Food delivery, hostels, India-specific cases, consumer SaaS/CRM and other unrelated domains return an empty collection. Philosophy retrieval uses specific passage tags such as career/autonomy, opposing arguments, revising beliefs and happiness/obligations. Mode alone or generic life-decision wording cannot force a citation. The AI-evaluation records do not establish outcomes for consumer SaaS or other startup categories. New topics need a newly inspected record; the service never fills gaps with fictional examples.

The AI-evaluation expansion uses complete, pinned official GitHub source snapshots with exact excerpts. Google's ADK overview supports defining objectives, critical tasks and metrics and examining both trajectory and final responses. Its criteria page distinguishes ROUGE-1 word overlap from rubric-based model judgments and documents sampling/majority voting. NIST Dioptra lists resource snapshots and experiment/input history as reproducibility and traceability properties. These are descriptions and recommendations from their publishers, not independent quality audits or commercial-outcome evidence. No numerical accuracy, market demand, failure probability, compliance guarantee or vendor superiority follows from them. The NIST record is not the complete AI RMF and does not certify conformance. Source dates are unknown rather than inferred from a Git commit.

Specific AI tags include `ai evals`, `ai evaluation`, `agent evaluation`, `evaluation metrics`, `llm judge`, `model testing`, `evaluation reproducibility` and `evaluation traceability`; `voice ai` is attached only to the general evaluation-objectives record, with an explicit limit that it does not measure speech, accents or latency. Consumer-product and motorcycle ideas may be stress-tested with explicitly labeled reasoning and questions, but this corpus supplies no efficacy, sensitive-skin safety, nutrition, cosmetic-claim compliance, helmet/accessory certification or local-law evidence. A concentrated-product claim such as “1 ml replaces 10 ml” remains the user's unverified hypothesis. Do not transfer AI/software citations to those products or treat absence of evidence as evidence of failure.

Each philosophy claim is marked `supportType: interpretation`: it describes a position in a particular passage and edition. Applying it to a user's circumstances belongs in a separately labeled interpretation, not in a claimed historical fact or empirical proof. When retrieval returns no records or all relevant records have been used, the conversation must present reasoning/questions with a visible coverage note and no sourced counterpoints. Retrieval excludes supplied `excludeIds` and returns at most three records.

## Server integration

`createEvidenceService()` in `src/lib/evidence/service.ts` implements the shared `EvidenceService`: `retrieve`, `get`, and `validate`. Construction excludes malformed, unreviewed, unsupported and hash-invalid records. Every return is cloned. Validation resolves IDs from the service's private canonical collection, never from the text/URL/claims in the retrieved copy sent to a model. Source text is untrusted data and must be delimited as such by the model adapter.

`validateCitations(output, retrieved, canonical)` validates structured output, response citations, state counterpoints and card counterpoints. Cited `counterpoint.text` **must equal the stored curated claim text**; a generated application to the user belongs in `counterpoint.interpretation` and the UI must label it as interpretation. A claim ID cannot decorate arbitrary factual prose. Citation IDs must be in the operation's retrieval set, the excerpt must belong to the claim, quotes must exactly equal an allowed excerpt, and paraphrases require permission without quote text. Model-supplied URLs/pages/extra authority fields are rejected by the strict schema. A response carrying a citation must contain its canonical claim or exact quoted excerpt. Unsourced surrounding prose remains model reasoning and must not be displayed as a sourced fact.

`resolveCitation(citation, canonical)` returns the source URL, author/work/edition/locator, exact quote or curated paraphrase, source limitations, and `Exact quote`/`Paraphrase` label. Its `supportType` additionally distinguishes an interpretation. Supply canonical records obtained through server-owned `get`; never resolve against browser/provider records. `validateMessageCitations(messages, retrieved, canonical)` applies the same rules to saved assistant messages. For retained state/cards/messages, the engine must supply the relevant server-owned historical retrieval set as well as any newly retrieved evidence; history does not grant authority to a provider-invented source.

## Stack Exchange sources

Posts from Stack Exchange sites (any host ending in `stackexchange.com`) are licensed CC BY-SA, so each record carries a structured `attribution`: the site name, a direct link to the post, every quoted author's display name linked to their profile on that site, and the licence name and legal-code URL. Pick the licence from the post date (UTC): before 2011-04-08 CC BY-SA 2.5, from 2011-04-08 to before 2018-05-02 CC BY-SA 3.0, from 2018-05-02 CC BY-SA 4.0. Where an answer was edited, use the date of the revision the excerpt comes from. The source dialog shows this attribution beside the excerpt and `/api/evidence/[id]` returns it, so the Markdown export can repeat it.

Stack Exchange excerpts are quoted word for word only: every claim has `paraphraseAllowed: false` and excerpts have `quoteAllowed: true`. This keeps the app from producing an adaptation of the post. `evidence:verify`, the import CLI and the evidence service reject a Stack Exchange record without attribution, with an attribution link outside Stack Exchange, or with any claim that allows paraphrase.

Add Stack Exchange material one reviewed post at a time. Open the question page, or fetch that single question through the Stack Exchange API, save the raw page or JSON as the source snapshot, and save the matching CC BY-SA legal code as the licence snapshot. Never use the data dump, an API bulk pull or scraping. Then follow the three-stage procedure below; author names and profile links come from the saved snapshot, and every excerpt must be an exact substring of it.

Philosophy Stack Exchange is the only Stack Exchange site in scope. Startups Stack Exchange closed in 2017, so its posts cannot carry the live links the licence requires. No Stack Exchange record is in the collection yet: on 2026-11-08 the build environment's network policy refused connections to philosophy.stackexchange.com, api.stackexchange.com and creativecommons.org, so no post or licence could be saved. The first reviewed set needs those hosts allowed, or snapshots saved by the owner by hand.

## Offline inspection and ingestion

Run `npm run evidence:verify`. It performs no network request and checks strict schemas, review chronology/metadata, every source and license file hash, exact excerpt substring equality, linked claims, unique IDs and path containment. Source/license paths must stay in `data/evidence/sources`; absolute paths, traversal and symlinks escaping the directory are rejected. Tests use the actual production snapshots and temporary corruption copies, never a fictional seed.

New material is added through three separate states. A reachable URL is never a verification result.

1. Inspect a legally distributable authoritative source and its license using normal TLS verification. Save a complete snapshot under `data/evidence/sources`, retain attribution notices, pin its version, and calculate source/excerpt/license hashes. Author a strict `EvidenceSchema` candidate with `status: draft`, null inspection/review timestamps and null reviewer. Make a matching inspection manifest using `InspectionEntrySchema`; its draft inspection timestamp is a placeholder until local inspection runs. Supported candidate claims remain untrusted drafts and are not retrievable.
2. Read the entire relevant passage/context, verify the edition/locator and review distribution terms. Import the draft with `npx tsx scripts/evidence-import.ts --draft /tmp/candidate.json --manifest /tmp/candidate-inspection.json --output drafts/candidate-draft.json`. No fetched page or imported record becomes reviewed. Then run `npx tsx scripts/evidence-import.ts --inspect data/evidence/drafts/candidate-draft.json --manifest data/evidence/drafts/candidate-draft.json.inspection.json --output drafts/candidate-inspected.json`. This checks local snapshot/excerpt integrity and records the inspection time; it does not attest that arbitrary authored claims follow from an excerpt.
3. Independently check every proposed claim against its supporting excerpt/context, domain tags and limitations. Remove unsupported causal claims, inferred pages/geography/dates and accidental modern editorial quotations. Run `npx tsx scripts/evidence-import.ts --review data/evidence/drafts/candidate-inspected.json --manifest data/evidence/drafts/candidate-inspected.json.inspection.json --reviewer 'named source reviewer' --output drafts/candidate-reviewed.json`. This is an explicit review attestation after inspection, not automated semantic verification. The CLI uses exclusive new-file writes and cannot replace the production seed. Promote reviewed records and matching manifest entries through a code review, then run `npm run evidence:verify` and `npx vitest run tests/evidence.test.ts`.

Full snapshots plus complete licenses/attributions occupy about 1 MB. The source attribution file is `data/evidence/sources/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Historical underlying passages are public domain in the United States; the electronic snapshots retain Project Gutenberg licensing/trademark rules, including fees and jurisdiction. Distribution of Gutenberg-branded text in the paid app requires assessment of those retained terms. Only reviewed historical passages are citation candidates; full source snapshots are provenance artifacts.

The source researcher could inspect GitHub source repositories but direct Gutenberg, GitHub Blog, Medium and several company sites returned HTTP 403. Their inaccessible contents are absent from the corpus and never labeled verified. On 2026-10-15 the official FDA cosmetics, FTC advertising-substantiation and NIST website URLs also returned proxy HTTP 403; no inaccessible page was added or labeled inspected. Reachable official Google and NIST GitHub source repositories supplied the AI-evaluation additions. Broader case coverage requires ordinary authorized source access and the same inspection/review process.
